Idle Trader ("the game") is developed by [your company / name] ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects and why.

What the game stores

Your game progress (level, cash, cars, homes, businesses, settings) is stored on your device. If you turn on the optional cloud save, that same progress is stored in our cloud database so you can restore it on another device. It contains no name, email or contact details unless you choose to sign in.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. Purchase records (which products you own) are handled by RevenueCat so your purchases can be restored; see RevenueCat's privacy policy. All in-game money is virtual: nothing bought in the game can be withdrawn or exchanged for real money.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS you can decline tracking when asked; on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. See Google's advertising policy. The "Remove ads" purchase turns off banner and interstitial ads; rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch them.

Notifications

With your permission, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your offline earnings are ready). They are created on your phone, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in the game's settings or your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can delete all game data with Me → Reset game, uninstall the app, and request deletion of any cloud save by contacting us.
